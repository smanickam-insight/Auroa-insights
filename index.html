Why now

Governance is a growth issue, not only a compliance one.

Four forces are converging at the same time. The question is no longer whether to govern AI, but how to scale it without recreating risk in every use case.

AI sprawl

Production bottlenecks

Regulatory obligations

Enterprise framework

From data governance to business-aware AI.

Trusted, business-aware AI combines governance, semantics and enterprise knowledge. Five connected framework dimensions support the six-phase delivery journey.

Govern: define the rules

Establish an AI Governance Board, responsible AI policies, risk classification, regulatory compliance and human oversight.

Understand: give data business meaning

Connect data, information and knowledge to canonical models, a common business vocabulary, ontologies and intelligence. Metadata management, discovery, catalogs, lineage, knowledge graphs and semantics help AI understand the business, not just the data.

Trust: prove reliability

Beacon supports profiling, validation and quality; Ethos supports bias, fairness, explainability and risk assessments; Insight supports observability, lineage, traceability and performance monitoring.

Operate: govern the full AI lifecycle

Ideate → Design → Build → Validate → Deploy → Monitor → Retire. Maintain model cards, risk assessments, approval records, audit trails and compliance reports throughout delivery.

Optimize: measure value and economics

Pulse connects AI FinOps, token economics, cost allocation, model utilization, ROI tracking and business value realization.

Strategic outcome

Data governance, canonical models, ontologies, Responsible AI and AI FinOps combine to enable trusted, explainable, business-aware AI: better decisions, improved trust, faster adoption and higher business value.

Framework alignment: EU AI Act, NIST AI RMF, India AI governance guidelines, ISO/IEC 42001, applicable data protection laws including GDPR and DPDP, and industry-specific requirements. Alignment supports evidence gathering; it does not itself establish certification or legal compliance.
