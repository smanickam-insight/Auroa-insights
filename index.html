AI-Powered Data Quality

Build trust into every data product.

Profile, validate, cleanse, score, and monitor enterprise data through an AI-assisted, metadata-driven quality framework.

  • • Automated profiling and reusable quality rules
  • • Quality scoring, anomaly identification, and reconciliation
  • • Audit, monitoring, and human-in-the-loop governance

MetaUI

Metadata becomes actionable.

Turn natural-language intent into validated, reviewable, and governed metadata proposals across connected Fabric and Databricks environments.

  • • Context-aware metadata proposals
  • • Row-level review and explicit human approval
  • • Discovery, lineage, quality context, and change history

SynGen

Generate realistic data, not another production copy.

Create schema-aware, relationship-consistent, and reusable synthetic datasets for engineering, QA, analytics, demonstrations, and AI.

  • • Dependency-aware, configurable generation
  • • Controlled negative testing and quality feedback
  • • Reusable jobs, previews, exports, and APIs
